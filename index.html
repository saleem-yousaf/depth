    SAME PRINCIPLES, DIFFERENT BOUNDARIES

    Location changes the implementation, not the need.

    01 / ON-PREMISES

    Control the estate.

    Separate the edge, workloads and data. Keep administration on its own network and protect the hardware itself.

    02 / CLOUD

    Identity is the perimeter.

    Private workloads, short-lived identities and policy as code. Write down who owns each side of the service boundary.

    03 / HYBRID

    Join systems, keep boundaries.

    Federate identity and encrypt the interconnect. A private route gives reachability; it does not grant trust.

    Architecture review: the questions worth asking

    01 Know your assets

    Map owners, data sensitivity, dependencies and trust boundaries before choosing controls.

    02 Plan for compromise

    Limit lateral movement, separate administration and make security logs hard to tamper with.

    03 Prove recovery

    Agree recovery time and point objectives, then restore into an isolated environment to prove them.

    04 Test the design

    Threat model the real flows and rehearse incident response. A diagram is where review starts.
